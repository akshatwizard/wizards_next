## Tone for "The story" and results — standing guidance

Keep it positive, and be upfront that digital marketing results are a long game — patience is part of the pitch, not an apology. State real numbers plainly and confidently; don't hedge every sentence with qualifiers. If a metric hasn't moved yet (e.g. clicks lagging behind rising impressions), frame it as a normal, expected stage of the process rather than a problem to explain away — and don't invent an improvement that didn't happen to avoid saying so.
